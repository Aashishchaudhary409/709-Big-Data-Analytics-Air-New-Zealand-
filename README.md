The aviation industry operates in a highly competitive and time-sensitive environment where operational efficiency directly impacts profitability, customer satisfaction and brand recognition. One of the most persistent challenges faced by airlines globally is flight delays, and it is estimated that these delays cost the industry billions of dollars each year. For airlines like Air New Zealand, which operates domestic as well as international flights, understanding the factors that drive delays and demand is imperative to maintain reliable service
This assessment applies big data frameworks, distributed MapReduce processing, and machine learning techniques to a dataset of 500,000 Air New Zealand flights that covers the full calendar year 2025. Basically, the analysis aims to uncover temporal patterns in flight volume and departure delays and also to build predictive and classification models that will help in quick operational decision-making. With the help of Databricks(Databricks Documentation, 2026) , Delta Lake and Spark, this project shows how large-scale airline data can be ingested, transformed and analysed effectively within a distributed computing environment.
